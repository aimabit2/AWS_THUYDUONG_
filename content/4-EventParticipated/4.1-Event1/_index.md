### General Information
- **Event name: FCAJ COMMUNITY DAY – "DATA DRIVEN, AI RISEN"**
- **Date and time: Saturday June 27, 2026 / 9:00 – 12:00**	
- **Location: Bitexco Financial Tower**	
- **Role: Attendee**	


### Main Content & Activities
The event featured 5 in-depth technical sessions focusing on modern cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence:

#### *9:00 – 9:25 AM | Session 1: Deep Response Engine: From Detection to Autonomous Resolution*
- **Speaker**: Steve Tran (CTO/Founder, CloudThinker)
- **Content**: Analyzed the complexity and challenges of modern cloud infrastructure management. Introduced the shift from traditional alert-driven systems to autonomous, action-driven AI engines. Featured a live demo showcasing AI capabilities in automatic incident resolution, cost optimization (FinOps), penetration testing, and log analysis to achieve zero-downtime.

#### *9:25 – 9:55 | Session 2: Voice Agents: Building Human-Like AI Conversations at Scale*
- **Speaker**: Nghi Danh (AI Engineer, Renova Cloud), Trung Vu (CEO, Revve AI), Kiet Tran (AI Engineer, AWS Student Builder Group).
- **Content**: Explored the evolution from rigid IVR systems and chatbots to natural, human-like voice conversations. Addressed challenges regarding low latency and accuracy using the speech-to-speech Amazon Nova Sonic foundation model and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrated with Amazon Bedrock.

#### *9:55 – 10:20 | Session 3: AWS DevOps Agent: Your Always-Available Operations Teammate*
- **Speaker**: Nguyen Nguyen & Bao Phan (Cloud Engineers, Cloud Kinetics)
- **Content**: Introduced a 24/7 virtual operations engineer designed to lower Mean Time to Detection (MTTD) and Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR). Demonstrated the use of the Bedrock AgentCore framework to orchestrate multi-agent collaboration and reasoning to diagnose and fix live failures on Amazon ECS.

#### *10:20 – 10:45 | Session 4: AI-Powered Productivity: Workforce Planning For Enterprise*
- **Speaker**: Truong Tran & Anh Dang (Solution Sales, Noventiq).
- **Content**: Discussed digital transformation challenges within Human Resources. Introduced the AI assistant Amazon Quick, highlighting features such as administrative automation, resume screening, and workforce analytics to support enterprise-level strategic planning.
#### *10:45 – 11:30 | Session 5: Building Secure Private MCP Connection with Amazon Quick*
- **Speaker**: Toan Nguyen (AWS Security Builder) & Nghi Danh (AI Engineer, Renova Cloud).
- **Content**: Analyzed data leakage risks when connecting internal corporate systems to AI via MCP. Provided a technical walkthrough on configuring private networking (VPC) to isolate environments for Amazon Quick. Shared practical insights on managing operational costs based on data transfer volume and query frequency.
